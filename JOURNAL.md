# Day 1 — Planning & CAD

Today I started my ESP32 DSLR camera project.

I worked on the initial planning and designed the first version of the camera body in CAD. I focused on the overall shape, dimensions, and where the main components will eventually be placed.

I don't have the hardware yet, so today's work was focused on the design and planning stage rather than assembling the electronics.

### Progress today

* Planned the basic camera architecture
* Created the initial CAD files
* Planned component placement
* Started organizing the project repository
* Planned the next steps for the electronics and firmware

Next, I'll refine the CAD design and work on the software and hardware architecture while I source the components needed for the physical prototype.
